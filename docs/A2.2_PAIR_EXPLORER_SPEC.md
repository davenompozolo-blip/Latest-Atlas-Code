Handoff spec for CC. Extends A2 (merged). Master spec §0 item 2.

**Scope:** a pair explorer on the regime tab, a corrected `ratio_pairs` metadata layer, and a
layout change to the page.

**Out of scope:** the phase-space tile (A2.3), the coherence read (still flag-off, unchanged),
anything in the theme layer.

and regime read are already gone.

---

## 1 · Why this exists

A2 shipped the axis layer with nothing beneath it. The panel says eleven pairs are three
things and then offers no way to look at a pair. This closes that floor.

The analytical point the explorer exists to make: **a ratio moving tells you the spread
moved, not why.** `XLI/XLU` rising because industrials rallied is risk appetite. The same
ratio rising because utilities sold off is a rates move hitting bond proxies. Opposite
meanings, identical ratio line. Plotting both legs against SPY is what separates them.

## 2 · `ratio_pairs` metadata — correct it first

### 2.1 — Rewrite `thesis`
`ratio_pairs.thesis` currently holds provisional descriptions. Replace with a plain-language
statement of **what the pair measures**, written to be read above a chart by someone who has
not memorised the taxonomy. One sentence, present tense, no jargon:

| pair_key | thesis |
|---|---|
| `xli_xlu` | Physical industrial expansion against defensive yield-seeking. |
| `hyg_tlt` | Appetite for credit risk against the safety of duration. |
| `xly_xlp` | Consumer willingness to spend against defensive staples demand. |
| `xle_xlu` | Energy-led inflation pressure against rate-sensitive defensives. |
| `xlf_spy` | Bank profitability and credit conditions against the broad market. |
| `qqq_spy` | Appetite for long-duration growth against broad equity beta. |
| `rsp_spy` | Breadth of participation against mega-cap concentration. |
| `dia_spy` | Old-economy blue chips against a tech-weighted market. |
| `iwm_spy` | Domestic small-cap risk tolerance against large-cap safety. |
| `eem_spy` | International growth and a weak dollar against US insulation. |
| `gld_spy` | Macro hedging demand against risk assets. |
| `cper_gld` | Global physical growth against monetary hedging. |

### 2.2 — Retire the `dimension` column
`dimension` holds the provisional groupings from the source documents. **A1 disproved three
of them**: `xle_xlu` is cyclical rather than safe-haven, `iwm_spy` is cyclical and dollar
rather than breadth, and the `growth_inflation` group splits across all three axes.

Keeping a contradictory parallel classification beside `factor_axis_loadings` guarantees a
future surface reads the wrong one. Either drop the column, or rename it
`dimension_deprecated` with a comment naming `factor_axis_loadings` as the successor and the
A1 study as the reason. **Do not repopulate it** with axis names — the axis mapping already
has a home and does not need a second one.

`cper_gld` has no loading on any axis. Its explorer entry renders with the axis field reading
"unassigned — loads below the noise threshold", not blank.

## 3 · Pair explorer

### 3.1 — Selection
Pair chips, **grouped by axis** with the axis name as a group label. This keeps the
three-axis structure visible while browsing eleven pairs; a flat row of chips teaches the
opposite of what A1 found.

`cper_gld` sits in its own "unassigned" group.

### 3.2 — Chart
For the selected pair, over a 60-session window:

- both legs, **rebased to 100** at the window start, from `adj_close`
- the ratio, rebased to 100, visually dominant (heavier stroke)
- **SPY, rebased to 100**, as a reference line in a neutral weight
- a dashed baseline at 100

SPY is not decoration. It is what lets the read establish whether either leg is actually
beating the market, and it is already in `market_prices`.

`adj_close` throughout. A price-only `xli_xlu` drifts down ~1.5%/yr on the dividend-yield
differential alone.

### 3.3 — Header
Above the chart, in this order:

1. Pair name.
2. **`thesis` — what the pair measures.** Read before the shape, not in a tooltip.
3. Axis name and the pair's **signed** loading.

The sign is load-bearing. `rsp_spy` loads **−0.47** on concentration: breadth loads negative
on an axis whose positive direction means narrowing. Without the sign displayed, a reader
sees a rising RSP/SPY chart beside a positive concentration score and concludes the panel
contradicts itself.

### 3.4 — Metric tiles
Five, computed over the window: ratio return, each leg's return, and **each leg's return net
of SPY**.

### 3.5 — The read
Generated, not authored. Three components:

**Direction and magnitude.** The ratio's move over the window.

**Which leg drove it.** The leg with the larger absolute move, and whether that was strength
or weakness. This is the part that distinguishes the two market states in §1.

**Whether it is real.** From the legs net of SPY:
- both legs beating SPY → rotation inside a rising market, not a defensive shift
- both legs lagging SPY → the ratio is sorting losers; treat the signal with care
- one beating → the spread carries genuine relative information

The middle case matters most and is the one a ratio-only view hides entirely.

End with a derivation line: loading, axis, that leg returns are net of SPY, window length.

### 3.6 — Window
60 sessions default. If a selector is offered, 20 / 60 / 120. Not required for v1.

## 4 · Layout

Regime tab, top to bottom:

1. **Pair explorer** — chips, chart, tiles, read.
2. **A toggled lower panel**, defaulting to intermarket axes:
   - **Intermarket axes** — the A2 panel, unchanged in content
   - **Macro dashboard** — the existing indicator dashboard, unchanged

One toggle, two states, axes selected on load.

**Noted for review after it ships:** this puts detail above summary. The axis panel is what
teaches that eleven pairs are three things, and the explorer may start reading as the primary
object. Ship as specified; revisit if the hierarchy reads wrong in use.

Both lower sections must be **present in the DOM and hidden by script after load**, not
conditionally rendered — a section that has never mounted cannot be measured for layout and
will jump on first toggle.
