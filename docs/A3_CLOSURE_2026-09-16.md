## 4 · A finding to keep: tariff is a false-positive generator as seeded

Not in the v0.1 report, found on 2026-09-16 while verifying the above, and the reason the
"1 of 4" headline understates how badly tariff does.

`tariff_rebasing` is **not** silent under v0.1-structural. It has **465 sessions** in
`emerging` or `established` — 160 emerging, 305 established — across **four** episodes:

| emerged | established |
|---|---|
| 2003-08-11 | 2003-10-07 |
| 2010-01-27 | 2010-03-25 |
| 2010-11-04 | 2011-01-03 |
| 2011-09-13 | 2011-11-08 |

**Every one is between 2003 and 2011. Its two target windows are 2018–19 and 2025, and it
detected nothing in either.** So the theme as seeded does not merely miss — it fires
confidently, four times, in periods it was not designed for, and stays silent in the two it
was. That is the ruling §4's own test failing in the sharpest possible way: *"A series that
fires everywhere is not a detector."*

This matters beyond the headline for one concrete reason: **F1 §3.1 sorts themes by
evidential weight — "themes that reached `established` first."** On today's data that sort
puts `tariff_rebasing` (305 established sessions) **above** `fiscal_dominance` (97), so the
theme with zero correct detections would have led the panel. A second, independent reason
the hold in §3 is the right call.

**No action taken on tariff.** Retiring it is a change to a seeded theme and needs its own
decision; the ruling authorises no further correction at this branch. Recorded here so the
next reader does not have to rediscover it.
